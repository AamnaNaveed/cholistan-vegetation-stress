# Modelling Climate-Driven Vegetation Stress in the Cholistan Desert

## Research Question
How do climate variability, soil properties, terrain, and land-cover characteristics influence seasonal vegetation stress in the Cholistan Desert, and how reliably can vegetation stress be predicted using satellite Earth observation and spatial machine-learning models?

## Study Area
Bahawalpur, Bahawalnagar, and Rahim Yar Khan districts, southern Punjab, Pakistan (encompassing the Cholistan Desert).

## Study Period
2018–2024 (Training: 2018–2023; Hold-out test: 2024)
